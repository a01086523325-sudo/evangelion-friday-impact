# 🎤 Friday Impact

> **"Get in the groove, Shinji!"**
> A fan-made Friday Night Funkin' (FNF) mod based on the iconic Neon Genesis Evangelion universe. The development is almost complete, bringing you fresh tracks, customized weeks, and unpredictable twists!

---

## 🌟 Key Features

*   **Character-Specific Story Weeks**
    *   🔵 **Rei Week**: Feel the rhythm hidden beneath her mysterious and calm demeanor.
    *   🔴 **Asuka Week**: High energy and tsundere charm! Prepare yourself for intense, fast-paced tracks.
    *   ⚪ **Kaoru Week**: Experience intricate charts that embody his deep and emotional bond with Shinji.
*   **🎂 Birthday Celebration Easter Egg**
    *   A special celebration event triggers under a specific condition (or on a certain date). Don't miss out on this hidden surprise!
*   **🚫 Unforgiving Anti-Cheat System**
    *   "Anta baka?!" If you dare manipulate the game files or use cheats, Asuka will personally step in to give you a harsh reality check and shut down your game. (You'd better play fair!)

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Download and extract the game archive.
2. Launch `PsychEngine.exe`.
3. Choose your desired week in Story Mode or Free Play and enjoy!
